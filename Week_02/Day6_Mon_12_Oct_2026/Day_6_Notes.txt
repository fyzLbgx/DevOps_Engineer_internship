Subnet Mask
------------
subnet mask tell us where the slip btw network and host part happen.
if we take a common ipv4 adress. lets say 192.168.1.10 with sunet mask of 255.255.255.0 or in cidr notation 192.168.1.10/24

192.168.1 is network part and 10 is host part

/24 means first 24 bits out of 32 go to network which caluclates to around 2^24 or 16,777,216 networks 
and remaining 8 bits calculates to 2^8 around 254 host deviecs

fiest adress in subnet is network and last one is broadcast
